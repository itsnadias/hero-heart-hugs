# The Brave Buddies Game Guide 🎮

Hi, super learner! 👋

This page tells you about all the little games you can play inside Brave Buddies. Every game is made to help you learn something new while having fun.

There are no wrong answers that make you feel bad — only chances to try again and learn more. Your buddies are always cheering you on! 🎉

## Your point party

When you play, you collect points! Here’s how it works:

- **Correct answer:** +10 points ⭐
- **Finishing an activity:** +20 points 🎉
- **Finishing a whole level:** +50 points 🏆
- **Finishing the whole adventure:** +100 bonus points 🌟

Don’t worry about being perfect. Every try counts!

## The games

### 1. Pick the Answer 🎯

You see a question and some buttons. Tap the one you think is right!

- If you get it right, your buddy says something like “Great job!” or “You got it!”
- If you pick a different one, your buddy gently explains why and invites you to try again.
- Example: *“Where are new blood cells made?”* — Inside bone marrow, in your hair, or in your shoes?

### 2. True or False? 👍👎

You read a sentence and decide if it is true or false.

- Tap the thumbs-up 👍 for true.
- Tap the thumbs-down 👎 for false.
- Your buddy tells you more about the answer, no matter what you picked.

### 3. Match the Pairs 🧩

You see two columns of words. Tap one item on the left, then tap the item on the right that goes with it.

- Match doctors with what they do.
- Match body parts with their jobs.
- Match feelings with kind ideas.
- When everything is matched, confetti celebrates with you!

The right side is jumbled up, so you have to think carefully. No peeking! 😊

### 4. Kind Choices 🌟

You see a bunch of picture cards with emojis. Some are helpful choices, and some are not.

- Tap the ones that feel kind to your body.
- Helpful choices turn green and give you a little note.
- Other choices turn soft blue and teach you why they might not be the best idea.
- Keep tapping until you find all the kind choices.

### 5. Body Explorer 🧍

You see body parts or body-helper words as picture cards. Tap the ones your buddy asks you to find.

- Some spots are special “highlights.”
- When you tap them, you learn a short note about that part.
- Find all the highlighted spots to finish the game.

### 6. Fun Facts with Pictures 🖼️

Between games, your buddy shows you friendly fact cards. They have big pictures — like cartoon X-rays, happy families, doctors, medicine, or cozy hospital scenes — so the ideas are easier to imagine.

You can tap **“Read to me”** if you want your buddy to read the fact out loud.

## Level finish fireworks

When you complete the very last game in a level, three colorful confetti poppers burst across the screen! 🎊

It’s our way of saying, “You did it!”

If you don’t like lots of motion, you can turn on **Reduced motion** in the settings. The confetti will take a little rest, but you still get to feel proud!

## The big finish

After all five levels, you get a surprise gift! 🎁 It might be:

- 🎨 A Rainbow Colouring Badge
- 🦸 A Super Learner Cape
- 🌟 A Star Explorer Sticker
- 🎵 A Happy Dance Ticket

Your gift is just for fun — it celebrates your curiosity, not anything medical.

## Always remember

You can learn, ask questions, and be kind to yourself. That’s what makes you a Brave Buddy!

If you want to know more about how the games are built, ask a grown-up to look at the code with you. The friendly files live in `src/components/adventure/`.
